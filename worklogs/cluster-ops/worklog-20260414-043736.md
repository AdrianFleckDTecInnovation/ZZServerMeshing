### Critical Finding: 5000 entities degrades the cluster

The coordinator journal shows that at 04:39:07 UTC (during or just after steady-scale at ~5000 entities), S3 missed a heartbeat and was marked dead:

```
WARN coordinator: S3 missed heartbeat -- marking as dead
```

What happened:
1. During steady-scale, S3 was receiving massive rebalance transfers (258+ entities at a time)
2. Under this load, S3's coordinator connection dropped or it failed to send a heartbeat within 5s
3. The coordinator marked S3 as dead and removed it from the registry
4. S3 continued running with its 685 authoritative entities, but was no longer part of the mesh
5. S3 had 0 shadows (no peers replicating to it) and 0 replication output (nothing to send to)
6. S1 and S2 continued operating as a 2-server mesh

The sim-server does NOT reconnect to the coordinator after being marked dead. This is the core issue.
