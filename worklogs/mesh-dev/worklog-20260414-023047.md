## Root Causes Found
The input-latency failure had three contributing causes:
1. **UDP channel saturation** (original report): Single 64-slot channel shared between bulk replication and latency-critical forwards. Fixed by splitting into two channels with priority.
2. **Stale player entity selection**: After crash-recovery, multiple player entities exist from prior test connections. The test picked `players[players.length - 1]` which was often on a different server, requiring forwarding that could fail. Fixed by diffing player lists pre/post spawn.
3. **Incomplete entity cleanup**: After steady-scale (10k+ entities), cleanup didn't fully drain before next test. Large entity counts caused WebSocket TCP backpressure that blocked MoveInput processing. Fixed by batched cleanup with metrics verification.
